Eight days, from Delhi to 2,800 metres.

This is the shape of your trip. Each stop is a day, plotted by altitude.

Swipe to see all eight days →

  1. Days 1–2 1,585 m

    Delhi to your houseboat

    The bus leaves Delhi for Jammu (or Katra) after 6 PM. Next day, drive on from Jammu to Srinagar, check in to your houseboat and take the day at leisure. Dinner included.

  2. Day 3 1,585 m

    Srinagar sightseeing

    Explore the Mughal Gardens and the beauty of Dal Lake, then Mall Road and other local spots. Don't miss the famous Rajesh Khanna shooting point. Night at a hotel. Breakfast and dinner included.

  3. Day 4 2,650 m

    Gulmarg

    The meadow of flowers: a summer resort, a ski resort in winter and home to the world's highest 18-hole golf course. Optional, at extra cost: the gondola to Khilanmarg (3,100 m), with Nanga Parbat in view on clear days, or on cloudy days a pony ride to Seven Springs and Alpather Lake (3,843 m), frozen until June. Night in Srinagar. Breakfast and dinner included.

  4. Day 5 2,800 m

    Sonamarg

    The meadow of gold at the head of the Sindh valley, ringed by pine-forested mountains up to 5,300 m. An optional pony ride, at extra cost, takes you to Thajiwas glacier, where snow stays all year. If snowfall closes Sonamarg, we visit Yousmarg instead. Night in Srinagar. Breakfast and dinner included.

  5. Day 6 2,740 m

    Pahalgam, Betaab and Aru

    Drive to Pahalgam for Betaab Valley, named after the Bollywood film shot there, and Aru Valley, a meadow that looks like the Swiss Alps under winter snow. Local transfers to Aru, Betaab and Chandanwari are covered. Night in Pahalgam. Breakfast and dinner included.

  6. Day 7 327 m

    Pahalgam to Jammu

    Breakfast, then the drive from Pahalgam to Jammu and the overnight bus back to Delhi.

  7. Day 8 216 m

    Back in Delhi

    Reach Delhi in the morning. Say goodbye to your new friends and start planning the reunion trip: there are so many places left to explore.

Questions people ask before booking.

Who is this trip for?

Glabol trips are only for 18–35 year olds, so you travel with a group of young people who are fun and easy to get along with. Getting friends to commit to a trip is hard, so plenty of people join solo when others back out. You may set off with a bunch of strangers, but you'll come back with a family.

Where does the trip start and end?

In Delhi. The bus leaves Delhi for Jammu (or Katra) after 6 PM on day 1, and you're back in Delhi on the morning of day 8. Bus tickets both ways are included. Flights to Srinagar and airport pickup or drop aren't part of the package.

Where do we stay?

Five nights on triple or quad sharing: one night on a houseboat in Srinagar, three nights at a hotel in Srinagar and one night in Pahalgam. Every hotel has a room heater or electric blanket, and the third and fourth person in a room get an extra mattress. Nights 1 and 7 are on the overnight bus.

Is Kashmir safe to travel right now?

The route sticks to Kashmir's main tourist circuit, and a dedicated tour captain stays with your group for the whole trip. If the weather, local government rules or the political situation change, Glabol rearranges the itinerary or transport as needed.

How fit do I need to be?

There are no treks on this trip. You travel by road and walk around each stop. In Gulmarg, vehicles stop at the parking area, so you continue on foot or by local union taxi. Pony rides, like the ones to Thajiwas glacier and Alpather Lake, are optional.

Is the Gulmarg gondola included?

No, it's optional and paid separately. Book your gondola tickets online as soon as you've booked the trip to avoid last-minute queues. Tickets depend on availability, and Glabol isn't responsible if they sell out. In winter, if heavy snow or black ice means only 4×4 vehicles are allowed, guests hire them at their own cost.

When is the best time to go?

Batches run from October to June, and all follow the same eight-day plan. December to February is snow season; if snowfall closes Sonamarg, we visit Yousmarg instead. Aru Valley turns into a winter wonderland, and Alpather Lake stays frozen until June.

What should I pack?

A down jacket comfortable down to −15°C, 2–3 woollen trek pants, 3 T-shirts, 2 woollen fleeces, waterproof woollen gloves, a sun cap, a woollen cap (plus a spare for layering), a woollen neck warmer or bandana, a quick-dry towel, 3–4 pairs of undergarments and 2 pairs of thermal innerwear. Carry an original ID (Aadhaar card, driving licence, voter ID or passport); there's no boarding without one. Pack light and leave valuables at home: Glabol isn't responsible if they're lost.

How do payment and cancellation work?

Pay the booking amount to confirm your seat and the rest at least 20 days before the trip, or the booking is cancelled automatically. Glabol also offers travel now, pay later; ask your trip captain how it works. The booking amount isn't refundable. Cancel 30–45 days before the start date and 50% of the trip cost is charged; within 30 days, 100%. You can move to another batch if you tell us at least 45 days before the trip, subject to availability.
